## What it does

Lights (epic HAS-256): Eaton lights through `amx-service`, Shelly lights, the real state, room rules driven by motion and presence, the empty house. Consumes `device.prod.light` on `/device` (HAS-257); knows the lights from the device registry (every 5 min, a light without `lightKind` skipped) and switches them through three drivers (HAS-258) - Shelly Gen1 `/relay`, `/light`, `/color`, Gen2 `Switch`, `Light`, `RGB` or `RGBW.Set` by the profile of the device, Eaton through `amx-service`; `POST /home/light/{id}?turn=`, `/{id}/brightness`, `/{id}/color`, `/rooms/{room}`; `NOT_FOUND_LIGHT`, `UNSUPPORTED_CAPABILITY`, `DEVICE_FAILED`, `REGISTRY_UNAVAILABLE`. Tracks the real state of every light (HAS-259) - Gen2 MQTT and `GetStatus` every 5 min, Gen1 `/status` every 60 s and `GET /home/light/events/gen1` (actions configured with HAS-265), Eaton status events and a status request of every Eaton light at the start; `light_state` (V1); `GET /home/light`, `/rooms/{room}`, `/{id}` - a field not known is left out. Occupancy (HAS-260): the presence sensors (Presence Gen4 by its zone, registry 91-93), the BLU Motion through the BTHome gateways, the Shelly Motion Gen1 by its actions and the Eaton PIR - occupied / empty / unknown per room, `lastMotionAt`, `illumination`, `lux`, in memory (unknown after a start); `GET /home/light/rooms/{room}/occupancy` and the same object in the room reads. Routed by path, every method (gateway 0.8.0, HAS-265); the Shelly Motion of the sanctum (103) calls `/events/gen1` through it. The rules of a room (HAS-261): `light_room_rule` and `light_motion_period` (V2) - auto-off, motion-on with periods per time of day, a lux gate, a manual hold, the switch of the resident (kept by a write of the administrator); `GET /home/light/rules`, `GET` / `PUT` / `DELETE /rooms/{room}/rules`, `PUT /rooms/{room}/rules/resident-enabled`; `INVALID_RULE` with the reason (a room on Eaton PIRs only gets no rule), `NOT_FOUND_RULE`. The engine (HAS-262): auto-off, motion-on, motion-off and the manual hold, at every fresh motion and every minute, commands with `source=rule`; the hold is derived from the state (a change not of `rule` / `empty-house` holds `manualHoldMinutes`), `heldUntil` in the light reads; every command logged in `light_rule_run` (V3), `GET /rooms/{room}/rules/runs?from=&to=` (at most 7 days). The empty house (HAS-263): presence-service every minute (unknown is never empty), the room rules pause while nobody is at home; by day the lights left on are switched off, by night (civil dusk to sunrise, `location-latitude` / `-longitude` of the manifest) a presence simulation from a stored plan (`GET` / `PUT /empty-house/plan`, V4, `INVALID_PLAN`) - phases evening / night / morning, seeded by the date, never against a light a person switched after the house emptied; `GET /empty-house`. The availability monitor (HAS-264): a Shelly light unreachable for 5 min, a presence sensor silent for 30 min, a battery motion sensor for 24 h - one alert a pass naming every device, a reminder every hour, an info on return (a light only once it answers again - HAS-296); open alerts in `light_availability_alert` (V5); Eaton lights and AMX PIRs not watched. An Eaton command whose status is not reported within 5 s is confirmed by a status request; a command of the rules the light did not take is sent once more, never a third time before a change (HAS-295). An Eaton light is known only once the AMX confirmed it (HAS-308): its restored state has no `on` until a frame or a status answer, `FRAME_SERVER_UNAVAILABLE` of amx-service makes every Eaton light unknown, the unconfirmed are asked every 5 min (one in turn as a probe when all are confirmed); auto-off also switches off a light not known to be off. A colour or level a device's own script set (source `loopback`) is no change - no hold, no row (HAS-269: the HALL UP lamps colour themselves once the rule switched them on). A BLU Motion still reporting motion keeps its room occupied - no auto-off, no motion-off, both delays from its clear; `motion` / `motionClearedAt` in the occupancy read, a motion never cleared ends after `motion-held-at-most` (PT60M) (HAS-349). Own database `home-automation-light` (pool 2), `Recreate`, never scaled
