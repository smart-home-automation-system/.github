## What it does

Hot water: reads a Shelly Uni every 3 min, keeps the flag `boiler-service` polls (on below 38 °C, off above 42), serves the dated reading and the stored history. The water meter of the house since HAS-316 (epic HAS-310): the Neo Smart Water Valve (registry 139) from the registry (`?kind=water valve`), its events from `device.prod.water` on `/device` (user `device`, `rabbitmq-password`) and `Shelly.GetComponents` every minute; `GET /home/water/valve` (state, l/min, bar, temperature, counter m3; a value older than PT5M left out); `water_meter_reading` a row on a change of the counter, `water_valve_status` one row (V3), 730 days kept; `GET /home/water/consumption?range=day|week|month|year&at=` (HAS-317: buckets of the hour, day or month, the total and the period before; a drop of the counter is a reset, never a negative volume; `INVALID_RANGE`); the bills of the waterworks under `/home/water/bills` (HAS-318: CRUD, each answered with the measured volume and the difference; `INVALID_BILL`, `NOT_FOUND_BILL`) and the daily history of the Shelly cloud imported once by hand under `/home/water/consumption/imported` (no cloud integration, `water_consumption_import`, V4); the alarms of the valve (HAS-319): no mains water (below 1 bar for 5 min), the valve not heard for 5 min, frost (water below 3 °C for 5 min) - an alert, hourly reminders, an info on the return, a time hysteresis on the return, open episodes in `water_alert` (V5), over a second connection to `/notification` (`notification-rabbitmq-password`); the flow alarms (HAS-320): above 25 l/min for 3 min, above zero for 30 min (a pause up to 1 min the same flow), above 0.5 l/min while the house is empty for 2 min - judged from every report of the rate (MQTT on a change, the RPC every minute; the litres of the last minute of the counter while the rate is stale), the empty house read every minute from presence-service (`GET /home/presence/residents/presence` over k8s DNS, unknown never empty), the kinds in `water_alert` (V6); the command of the valve (HAS-321): `POST /home/water/valve?state=open|close&source=` - `Boolean.Set` of `boolean:200` sent once, confirmed by the report of the valve within 60 s (`lastCommandConfirmed` in `GET /valve`, the third unconfirmed in a row an alert), `water_valve_command` (V7); `water.flow.close-on-alarm` (off - the owner) lets the continuous flow and the empty house close it, the high flow never. The circulation pump of the hot water (epic HAS-403, HAS-404): relay 1 "CIRCULATION" of the same Uni read every 15 s (every 5 s during a run), `GET /home/water/circulation`, `POST /home/water/circulation?source=` (the tank below 38 °C is `409 TANK_COLD`; `/relay/1?turn=on&timer=180` sent once - the timer replaces the `auto_off` 60 s of the device), stopped at the return of 35 °C (sensor 1, on the circulation - checked on the first run 2026-11-06) or after 3 min, `GET /circulation/runs`, `circulation_run` (V8, 365 days); `DEVICE_FAILED` is "Device failed" since. The automation of the circulation (HAS-405, the scene of the Shelly cloud disabled at its deploy): a new motion or a light switched on in the bathrooms (`GET /home/light/rooms/{room}` of light-service over k8s DNS every 10 s) and every minute of the comfort windows (06:00 - 08:00, 19:00 - 23:30) start a run while somebody is at home (presence-service, unknown never), the tank at 38 °C, the return below 30 °C, 20 min after the last run stopped by the temperature or the limit, outside 23:30 - 05:30; `water.circulation.automation.enabled` switches it off; three runs from a cold return stopped by the limit that raised the return by less than 1.5 °C are an alert (owner 2026-11-06 - by the rise; the return rarely reaches 35 °C in 3 min). The water softener (epic HAS-396, HAS-397): a Viessmann AquaHome 20 Smart with no local API, read every 5 min from the cloud of the iQua application (`api.myiquaapp.com`, the account in the secret `water` - `iqua-email` / `iqua-password`; three failed logins hold the logins for 30 min - the cloud blocks an account above about 1000 calls a day), `GET /home/water/softener` (water, salt - the per cent the application shows, from `enriched_data`, regenerations, hardness, alerts; `503 SOFTENER_UNAVAILABLE` before the first reading); its history since HAS-398 - `softener_reading` a row on a change, `softener_regeneration` from the regeneration events of the cloud every hour (V9, 730 days), `GET /home/water/softener/history?metric=&from=&to=` (at most 366 days, `UNKNOWN_METRIC`), `/softener/days` (litres per day), `/softener/regenerations`; its alerts since HAS-399 - low salt (warn, daily), leak / shutoff valve / resin / error (error, hourly), excessive use, not heard from the cloud for `water.softener.offline-after` (PT30M, warn) as episodes of `water_alert` (V10), `GET /home/water/softener/alerts` (the list of the cloud, kept 5 min), and a continuous or empty-house flow of a regeneration (`regen_status_enum`, `regen_time_secs` 02:00) judged from its end (the words of the cloud `regeneration_status` decide - none / scheduled are none; `regen_status_enum` 1 is scheduled); since HAS-400 the one write on the softener - `POST /home/water/softener/regeneration?when=now|tonight`, `DELETE` to cancel one scheduled (`PUT /devices/{id}/command`, the pairs the device offers; one command a minute; `REGENERATION_IN_PROGRESS`, `COMMAND_TOO_SOON`, `SOFTENER_COMMAND_FAILED`); since HAS-401 its live flow - `PUT /home/water/softener/live` holds the WebSocket of the cloud (`GET /devices/{id}/live`, the address with its token never logged) for `water.softener.live-for` (PT5M), `DELETE` closes it, the frames of the flow update `GET /softener` (`live`, `liveUntil`), `429 LIVE_THROTTLED`. State in memory: `Recreate`, never scaled
